Questions

Is the first call really free?

Yes. It's 20 minutes, it costs nothing, and you don't have to buy anything after it.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how much help you want and how close your deadlines are. After the free call we send a written plan with the price, before any work starts. You can also pay for a single essay review or one session.

Can you guarantee I get in?

No, and nobody honest can. Admissions decisions belong to the colleges. What we can promise is a clear plan, honest feedback and applications you're proud of.

Do you write essays?

Never. You write every word. We ask questions, help you find the story and give feedback on your drafts. Colleges treat essays written by someone else, or by AI, as fraud, and so do we.

You're a college student. Why should we trust you?

I'm not a former admissions officer and I don't claim inside knowledge. I went through this process last year, I read carefully, and I'll tell you the truth about your application. If your family wants a credentialed counselor, look for IECA or HECA members.

Is it too late if I'm a senior?

Usually not. Regular decision deadlines are mostly in early January, and there's a lot we can do in a few weeks. Book a call and we'll tell you honestly what's realistic.

Who signs up, me or my parents?

Students can book the free call. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian signs the agreement and handles payment, and they're copied on our emails.

Where do calls happen?

Online, over Google Meet or Zoom, so it doesn't matter where you live.

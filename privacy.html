Privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

What we collect. When you book a call or email us, we get what you give us: your name, email, grade, the schools you're thinking about and anything you write in your message. Our booking tool (Calendly) also records the time you picked.

How we use it. Only to set up and run your calls, answer your questions and, if you become a client, do the work you asked for. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't use it for ads.

Who else sees it. Calendly handles booking and Google handles our email. Each has its own privacy policy. Nobody else gets your information unless the law requires it.

Students under 18. If a student under 18 becomes a client, a parent or guardian signs the agreement and is copied on our emails. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Calls. We never record a call without asking everyone on it first.

Your choices. Email us any time to see, correct or delete what we have about you, and we will do it.

Do Not Track. This site doesn't use tracking cookies or analytics, so it treats every visitor the same whether or not their browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Changes. If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top.

Contact. Email us.

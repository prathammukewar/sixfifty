The senior year checklist

Every step of a college application on one page. Tap a box to check it off, or print it. Dates are typical; always confirm each college's own deadline on its website.

Your list

  • Write down what you want: size, location, majors, cost.
  • Pick 8 to 12 schools: a few reaches, several targets, at least 2 likely schools you'd be happy at.
  • Run each school's net price calculator with a parent.
  • Decide whether to apply Early Decision (binding) anywhere.

Testing and recommendations

  • Check each school's test policy: required, optional or test blind.
  • Send official scores where required.
  • Ask two teachers for recommendations at least a month before your first deadline.
  • Give your counselor and teachers a short brag sheet.

Activities

  • List everything you've done since 9th grade, then pick your top 10.
  • Put the most important activity first.
  • Write each description with numbers: hours, people, results.
  • Pick up to 5 honors for the Common App.

Essays

  • Choose a Common App prompt and write a messy first draft. The limit is 650 words.
  • Make a list of every supplement for every school, with word counts.
  • Write "why us" essays with details you can only find for that one school.
  • Get feedback from one or two people, not ten.
  • Read every essay out loud before you submit.

Deadlines

  • Nov 1 or Nov 15: most Early Decision and Early Action deadlines.
  • Oct 1 onward: FAFSA and CSS Profile open. File early; some aid is first come.
  • Jan 1 to Jan 15: most Regular Decision deadlines.
  • Put every deadline, and the date you want to finish a week before it, in one calendar.

Before you hit submit

  • Preview the PDF of each application.
  • Check that the right school name is in every "why us" essay.
  • Pay or request a fee waiver.
  • Make an account on each school's applicant portal and check it for missing documents.
